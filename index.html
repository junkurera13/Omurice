A trial for line cooks

One plate. Beat the eggs, scramble, tap them into a football, and lay it on the rice. Then the head chef slices it open. Score over 90 and you're hired.

@

Chef Okada's recipe card

  1. Beat three eggs until no white streaks are left. Fish out any shell.
  2. Heat the pan on medium-high. Add 10–20 g butter. Pour when it foams. Brown butter is too late.
  3. Scramble hard straight away, all over the pan, for about 8–12 seconds. Small soft curds.
  4. Stop when it looks like wet scrambled egg. Let the bottom set into a thin skin. Turn the heat down if you need time.
  5. Fold. Tilt the pan away and tap your wrist so the egg jumps and rolls over itself into an American-football shape. Raw skin tears. Overcooked egg cracks.
  6. Plate it seam-down in the middle of the rice. I cut it. If the inside is golden and runny it slides open and covers the rice. If it's solid, you're not hired.

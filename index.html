THE TAMPOPO OMELETTE: HOW WE DO IT HERE
========================================

The egg is a soft football of barely-set egg.
It sits on the rice. You slit it end to end,
it falls open, and the creamy middle spills
down over the rice. "Hanjuku" = half-set.

1. BEAT
   3 eggs. Beat until there are no streaks of
   white. Don't whip air into it. Pick out any
   shell.

2. HEAT + BUTTER
   Medium-high. Add a pat of butter (10-20 g).
   Wait for it to melt and FOAM. Pour while it
   foams. Brown butter = too late. Smoke = way
   too late.

3. SCRAMBLE
   The second the egg hits the pan, stir hard
   with the chopsticks. Fast little circles,
   all over the pan. This makes small soft
   curds. About 8-12 seconds.

4. STOP
   When it's like wet scrambled egg on top, STOP
   stirring. Let the bottom set into a thin skin
   for a few seconds. Turn the heat down if you
   need time.

5. ROLL
   Tilt the pan away from you. Tap the handle
   so the omelette folds over itself toward the
   far edge. Keep tapping until it's a football.
   Skin too raw = it tears. Too cooked = it
   cracks.

6. PLATE + CUT
   Slide it onto the middle of the rice. One cut
   along the top, tip to tip. If you got it
   right it opens up and covers the rice.

Don't let it brown. Pale yellow only.
Total time in the pan: well under a minute.

                                   - Chef Okada
